# Third-party material

## Test fixtures — `corpus/corpus.json`

Generated by `scripts/fetch-corpus.ts` from three upstream projects, each
pinned to an exact commit. The file holds their test fixtures: strings they
assert a detector must match, and — the reason we want them — strings they
assert it must **not**.

Nothing in `hooks/` is derived from these projects. Our rules remain our own;
only the fixtures they are measured against are theirs.

### gitleaks — MIT

- `https://github.com/gitleaks/gitleaks`, commit `b58d3f102cf3a2c84cb7f923d05c25c9b1aed84b`
- Taken: the `tps` / `fps` string slices in `cmd/generate/config/rules/*.go`,
  and `DefaultStopWords` from `cmd/generate/config/rules/stopwords.go`.
- Upstream warns that the generator API is not stable, which is why the commit
  is pinned rather than tracked.

### Nosey Parker — Apache-2.0

- `https://github.com/praetorian-inc/noseyparker`, commit `2e6e7f36ce36619852532bbe698d8cb7a26d2da7`
- Taken: the `examples:` and `negative_examples:` lists in
  `crates/noseyparker/data/default/builtin/rules/*.yml`.
- Copyright Praetorian Security, Inc. Licensed under the Apache License,
  Version 2.0: `http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0`.
- **Changes made:** the example strings are extracted from their YAML and
  stored in `corpus/corpus.json` alongside fixtures from other projects. The
  strings themselves are unmodified. No Nosey Parker source code is included.

### secretlint — MIT

- `https://github.com/secretlint/secretlint`, commit `c0b8bc16dd8da80b0add80b1ddbf863e2987fce2`
- Taken: `packages/@secretlint/secretlint-rule-*/test/snapshots/{ng,ok}*/input.txt`,
  as whole documents. Snapshots suppressed by secretlint's own configuration
  (`ok.allows`, `ok.filePathGlobs-no-match`, `ok.pattern-allows`) are excluded:
  those files do hold a credential and the rule was merely told to ignore it,
  which says nothing about detection.
- MIT License, Copyright (c) 2020 azu. Same permission and warranty text as
  above.

## Detection heuristics — `hooks/detect/suppress.ts`

Suppression shapes and the binary skip list adapted from
`ray-amjad/awesome-claude-code-function-hooks` (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Ray
Amjad).

## Not used, and why

- **trufflehog** (`trufflesecurity/trufflehog`) is AGPL-3.0. Copying its
  detectors or its test values into this plugin would put osm under AGPL.
- **semgrep-rules** ships under the Semgrep Rules License v1.0, which is not an
  open-source licence; its secrets content is largely a repackaging of
  gitleaks, which we take from the source under MIT.
- **mazen160/secrets-patterns-db** is CC-BY-SA-4.0 (share-alike) and carries no
  fixtures.
